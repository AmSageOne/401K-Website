# 401(k) Rollover Challenge — Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 18, 2026
Entity: King Tides Ventures LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how King Tides Ventures LLC ("we," "us") collects and uses information through the 401(k) Rollover Challenge.

**1. Information we collect**
When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you provide it, your name. We also automatically collect basic usage data (such as pages visited and general location) via analytics tools like Google Analytics.

**2. How we use it**
We use your email to send the five-day Challenge series and, if you opt in, occasional follow-up messages about coaching or related programs. We use aggregated site analytics to understand how visitors use the site and improve it.

**3. What we never collect**
We never ask for or store retirement account login credentials, passwords, or account numbers through this site or the Challenge emails.

**4. Sharing**
We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us send email and process signups — such as our email service provider and other software tools we use to operate the service — solely to operate the service.

**5. Your choices**
You can unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in every message, or contact us to request that we delete your information.

**6. Cookies and analytics**
This site uses cookies and Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

**7. Contact**
Questions about this policy can be sent to the reply address on any Challenge email, by email at info@kingtides.io, or by mail to: King Tides Ventures LLC, 280 Spear St., Ste. 901, San Francisco, CA 94105.
